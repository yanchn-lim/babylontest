About this lighting study

Four diffuse bounces use the same 1,024-ray transfer method as the comparison scene. Time and light changes update the lighting; walking does not restart it. Seven fixed room lights can be switched automatically or manually. The original textured materials are retained.

This is a first apartment prototype. Glass passes light without tint or refraction in the diffuse calculation. Glossy indirect reflections are not included. The older baked skylight is the loading and WebGL fallback; it cannot reproduce changing indirect sun or lamp light. Mobile performance is not yet measured.
