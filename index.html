Day / night

Manual sun direction

Time controls sun direction and warmth. Brightness sliders set daytime levels; the cycle fades sky, baked light, reflections and shafts at night. Playback starts paused on reload. This is an artistic path, not a geographic solar clock.

Sunlight

Direction moves the sun and its shadows. Warmth shifts sunlight from neutral to warm.

Interior fill

Baked indirect lighting. No real-time GI rendering cost.

Baked light controls fixed skylight. Diffuse fill adds ambient light; environment brightness also affects reflections. Balanced lighting restores exposure and fill levels.

Shadows

Map size controls detail. Filter quality controls sampling cost. Softness applies to PCSS only.

Increase bias to reduce surface stripes. Too much can detach shadows.

Refine each surface

Tune a material and see the result in the scene.

Surface finish

Changes apply to every surface using this material. Normal detail requires a normal map. Reflections use the existing environment. Baked lighting remains fixed while you tune materials.

Finish the image

Adjust the camera response, glow and atmosphere.

Image & atmosphere

Lower threshold makes more surfaces glow. Larger radius spreads the glow and can cost more GPU time.

Scattering changes how strongly shafts appear when looking toward the sun.

Shafts are independent of surface shadows. Environment brightness does not change baked skylight.
