What this experiment measures

Cached diffuse reuses 1,024 offline ray samples per surface point to preserve the dense-ray lighting result. It starts with four indirect diffuse bounces. Radiance cascades remain available for comparison; choose one bounce to reproduce the approved cascade checkpoint. Both update when time, lamps or bounce count change and stay fixed while walking. The baked baseline blends offline lighting maps between three-hour checkpoints and is the WebGL fallback. All methods use the same live direct lights, shadows, exposure and Cycles references.

Cached diffuse downloads about 39 MB of prepared data for this fixed room. Baked lighting stays visible while it loads. This is a visual prototype: Local reflections add a filtered room capture; switch them off to compare with the diffuse-only checkpoint. They update after lighting changes and stay fixed while walking. Cycles computes more complete light paths and reflections. Mobile performance has not been established.
