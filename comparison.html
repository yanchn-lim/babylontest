About the renderer

Cached diffuse lighting uses 1,024 prepared rays per surface sample and four indirect bounces. Lighting updates when time or fixed lights change and remains stable while walking. The room retains its matched offline Cycles references and uses baked lighting automatically when WebGPU is unavailable. Furniture scenes require WebGPU for indirect lighting.

Local reflections are enabled in the room. The furniture scenes retain their diffuse study settings. The ÄPPLARYD sample correction reduces artifacts around overlapping cushions; some seams remain unresolved. Offline preparation is required after scene changes. Mobile performance and Cycles parity have not been established.
