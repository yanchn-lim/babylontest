What this experiment measures

Radiance cascades compute sky lighting and one diffuse bounce from the full scene. Lighting updates when time or lamps change and stays fixed while walking. The baked baseline blends offline lighting maps between three-hour checkpoints. Both use live direct sun and lamp shadows and the same Cycles references, exposure and display curve. Baked lighting remains visible while cascades prepare and is the WebGL fallback.

This is an initial visual study, not accepted renderer parity. The cascade study has finite spatial and directional resolution; Cycles includes more bounces and glossy reflections. Local glossy reflections are not implemented in Babylon. Mobile performance has not been established.
