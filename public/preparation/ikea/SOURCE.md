# IKEA preparation fixture

Original GLBs selected from Injaneity's qualified IKEA Singapore catalogue at
commit `9b9eb48e0a44a322abcc55b9deae93637682967c`. Downloaded 2026-09-18 from the
exact IKEA asset URLs recorded in `manifest.json`. That manifest includes article
numbers, product links, dimensions, triangle counts and SHA-256 file hashes.

The nine additional products contain 448–2,988 triangles each. Eighteen placed
copies total 28,550 triangles. The existing ÄPPLARYD 99528173 sofa contributes
7,628 triangles, for 36,178 furniture triangles across 19 placed products.
The sofa's original asset and provenance remain in `public/comparison/applaryd`.

The fixture retains original geometry, materials and textures. Placement and
dimension alignment are applied through transforms. It does not use decimation
or generated lighting proxies. IKEA and its suppliers retain their rights;
this repository grants no new licence to these assets.
